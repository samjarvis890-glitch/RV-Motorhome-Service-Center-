PREPARATION CHECKLIST

A BETTER REQUEST STARTS WITH BETTER DETAILS.

Having these 6 specifications ready ensures rapid evaluation, exact parts pre-ordering, and accurate bay allocation.

01 COACH SPECS

RV TYPE & LENGTH

Make, model, model year, overall bumper-to-hitch length, chassis platform, and slide-out configuration.

02 CURRENT SITE

LOCATION

ZIP code, storage facility status, campground site number, or residential pad access conditions.

03 PRIMARY FOCUS

SERVICE NEED

Specific symptoms, warning lamps, system failure history, or scheduled mileage interval.

04 SCHEDULE

TIMING & DEPARTURE

Target trip departure date, campground check-out timeline, or seasonal winterization deadline.

05 SYMPTOM CONTEXT

DESCRIPTION

Detailed notes on when the issue occurs, previous technician repairs, or recent battery upgrades.

06 DIAGNOSTICS

PHOTOS & DATA PLATES

Clear photos of vehicle VIN tags, appliance model serial stickers, roof lap seals, or fault displays.
